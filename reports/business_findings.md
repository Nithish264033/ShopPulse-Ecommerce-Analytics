\# ShopPulse – Business Findings



\## 1. Introduction



ShopPulse is an e-commerce business intelligence project developed using the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset. The project analyzes sales performance, customer retention, and delivery efficiency to identify important business patterns and provide data-driven recommendations.



\## 2. Revenue Analysis



\- Total product revenue from delivered orders: ₹13,221,498.11.

\- Total freight revenue: ₹2,198,275.64.

\- Delivered orders: 96,478.



\*\*Finding:\*\*



The platform generated significant product revenue from delivered orders. Freight charges also contributed to the overall transaction value. Monitoring monthly revenue can help identify sales trends and support business planning.



\## 3. Customer Retention Analysis



\- Total unique customers: 96,096.

\- One-time customers: 93,099.

\- Repeat customers: 2,997.

\- Repeat customer rate: 3.12%.



\*\*Finding:\*\*



Most customers placed only one order, while a relatively small percentage returned for additional purchases. This indicates an opportunity to improve customer retention through loyalty programs, personalized offers, and customer engagement campaigns.



\## 4. Delivery Performance Analysis



\- Average delivery time: 12.56 days.

\- Late deliveries: 7,826.

\- Late delivery rate: 8.11%.



\*\*Finding:\*\*



The average delivery time was 12.56 days. Approximately 8.11% of delivered orders were late. Improving logistics coordination and monitoring delayed shipments could help improve customer satisfaction.



\## 5. Business Recommendations



1\. \*\*Improve Customer Retention:\*\* Introduce loyalty programs and personalized discounts to encourage repeat purchases.

2\. \*\*Optimize Delivery Operations:\*\* Identify causes of delivery delays and improve logistics coordination.

3\. \*\*Monitor Revenue Trends:\*\* Use monthly revenue analysis to understand sales performance and support planning.

4\. \*\*Improve Customer Experience:\*\* Focus on timely delivery and better post-purchase engagement.



\## 6. Conclusion



The ShopPulse analysis highlights three important business areas: revenue performance, customer retention, and delivery efficiency. The findings show that customer retention and delivery performance offer opportunities for improvement.



The Power BI dashboard provides interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring to help users understand business performance and make data-driven decisions.
